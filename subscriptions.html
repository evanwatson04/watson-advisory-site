Why ongoing

The value of being known

A one-time filing gets the return done. An ongoing relationship means that when something changes — a new client, a big expense, a business decision — there's someone already familiar with your situation to call.

  • Tax planning happens throughout the year, not in April
  • Books are clean month-to-month, not scrambled at year-end
  • Questions get answered fast by someone who knows your situation
  • Proactive advice — things flagged before they become costly
  • No re-explaining your situation to a new person each year

Flexible by design

Subscription plans aren't rigid packages. If you need more one month and less another, that can be accommodated. If your business grows and your needs change, the scope can be adjusted.

One-time projects are always available too — subscriptions are for clients who want consistency, not a requirement to work together.

The best first step is a quick conversation to figure out what structure actually makes sense for your situation.
