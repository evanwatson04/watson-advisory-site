About special projects

One-off work, built to spec

These are standalone projects — not ongoing subscriptions. Pricing is quoted per project after you describe what you need. Even a rough description is enough to start.

Special projects come from clients who have a specific problem that doesn't fit a standard service — a strategy they want to test before using real capital, a spreadsheet they've been meaning to build, or a data set that needs to be structured before it's useful.

Tools and experience include Excel (advanced formulas, VBA, Power Query), Python (pandas, backtesting libraries, data manipulation), and financial analysis. The right tool is chosen based on the project — not forced into a preferred format.

Trading Strategy Backtesting

Test your strategy before risking real capital

Have a trading strategy — technical indicators, entry/exit rules, position sizing — and want to see how it would have performed historically? A backtest builds that model and runs it against real historical data to evaluate performance, risk, and reliability.

  • Strategy logic built to your specifications
  • Historical performance analysis (return, drawdown, win rate)
  • Risk metrics — Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, volatility
  • Equity curve and trade log
  • Plain-English summary of results and limitations
  • Excel or Python delivery depending on complexity
Request a backtest quote
What to include in your quote request
Describe the strategy rules (entry, exit, position sizing), the asset or market (stocks, ETFs, crypto, etc.), the time period, and what questions you're trying to answer. No code required — just describe the logic in plain terms.
Example deliverables
  • Moving average crossover backtest on SPY (5yr)
  • RSI mean-reversion strategy vs. buy-and-hold
  • Multi-asset rotation model with monthly rebalancing
Personal Finance Tracker

A budget spreadsheet that actually fits your life

Generic budget templates aren't built around your income, expense categories, or goals. A custom personal finance tracker is built specifically for your situation — so maintaining it feels natural and the numbers are actually meaningful to you.

  • Income and expense tracking by your categories
  • Monthly and annual budget vs. actual view
  • Net worth tracker (assets, liabilities, trend)
  • Savings and goal progress tracking
  • Charts showing spending trends over time
  • Built to be maintained by you going forward
