About Evan

Evan Watson is the sole proprietor behind Watson Advisory Services. He provides direct service - no layers and no handoffs.

Background includes tax preparation, audit work, bookkeeping cleanup, Excel reporting and automation including VBA, and financial modeling/pro forma work. He focuses on helping owners reduce taxes legally, improve profitability, and make better decisions with clearer reporting.

From Pell City, Alabama. Working primarily in the Jackson metro while attending Millsaps. Primary service areas are central Alabama and central Mississippi, with clients from anywhere welcome.

Many clients want someone who can bridge the gap between traditional operations and newer technology. That is a major part of the service: translating numbers, cleaning up systems, and making the work easier to manage.
