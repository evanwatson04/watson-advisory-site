About Evan

Evan Watson is an accounting student at Millsaps College with hands-on experience in tax preparation, bookkeeping, financial analysis, auditing, and small business consulting.

He has worked as a Business Analyst with ElseWorks, supporting entrepreneurs and small businesses through financial analysis, data modeling, reporting, and strategy work. He has also gained bookkeeping and operations experience with Mississippi Cold Brew Coffee & Tea Company, using QuickBooks and Excel in real business settings.

Evan also completed an audit internship with the Mississippi Office of the State Auditor and served as a VITA tax preparer, preparing federal and state returns for individuals and families. He is currently pursuing his B.B.A. in Accounting at Millsaps College and has been consistently recognized for strong academic performance.

Through Watson Advisory Services, Evan provides direct and practical support for clients who need help with taxes, cleanup work, forecasting, analytics, and financial organization.
