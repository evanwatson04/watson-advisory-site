Experience & approach

Bookkeeping that actually serves your business

Bookkeeping experience spans beverage businesses, home-care franchises, nonprofits, and self-employed individuals. Past work includes full cleanup engagements — taking books from months or years behind and reconciling everything from scratch — as well as ongoing monthly support for businesses that want consistent, owner-ready financials every month.

The focus is on making the records actually useful — not just technically correct. That means reports you can read, categories that make sense for your business, and books that support better tax filing when the time comes.

  • Bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Monthly close and owner-ready financial summaries
  • Catch-up and back-period cleanup
  • QuickBooks and Excel-based workflows
  • Books structured to support tax preparation
  • Reporting improvements and structure overhaul
